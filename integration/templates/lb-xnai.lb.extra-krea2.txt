## Krea2 Character Identity Profiles

Use this lorebook only for physical identity that must remain stable between images.
The illustration planner copies each matching profile into the `appearance` field.
Do not put scene, pose, camera, lighting, background, or temporary clothing here.

### Character: CHARACTER_NAME

Canonical name: CHARACTER_NAME

Fixed appearance: Write one complete English paragraph describing the character's
age category, skin, face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, hair color and style,
body shape, and any permanent identifying features. Avoid prompt weights, tags,
LoRA names, camera language, and negative prompts.

Continuity notes: List only permanent details that the planner must never change.
